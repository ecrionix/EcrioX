What is HEIC and Why Convert to JPG?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the modern image format adopted by Apple starting with iOS 11. Built upon the HEVC (H.265) compression standard, HEIC produces photos that are roughly half the file size of an equivalent JPG while maintaining identical or superior visual quality and supporting 16-bit wide color gamuts.

Despite its compression advantages, HEIC suffers from a major drawback: limited compatibility. Older versions of Windows, Android devices, photo-editing tools, e-commerce platforms, and government application portals frequently reject .heic and .heif files. Converting your iPhone photos to standard JPG or PNG ensures they can be opened, viewed, and shared everywhere.
