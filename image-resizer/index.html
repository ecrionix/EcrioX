How to Resize Images Online with EcrioX

  1. Upload your image: Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP photo into the workspace.
  2. Set your dimensions: Enter target pixel values for width and height, or select quick percentage buttons (such as 50% or 75%).
  3. Choose Social Media Presets: Quickly scale photos for Instagram, YouTube thumbnails, Facebook, or Twitter headers.
  4. Lock Aspect Ratio: Keep the aspect ratio locked to prevent unintended image stretching or distortion.
  5. Download: Click "Download Resized Image" to save the file instantly to your device.

High-Quality Interpolation & Smoothing

Rescaling pixel dimensions without proper interpolation can produce jagged edges (aliasing) or blurry artifacts. EcrioX leverages advanced HTML5 Canvas rendering with imageSmoothingQuality = 'high', applying smooth bilinear and bicubic sampling algorithms to preserve fine lines, sharp text, and natural skin tones.

Format Selection & Transparency Support

  • PNG (.png): Recommended for logos, icons, and illustrations where transparent backgrounds must be maintained.
  • WebP (.webp): Next-generation image format offering up to 30% smaller file sizes than JPEG with identical visual fidelity.
  • JPEG (.jpg): Best for standard photographs, gallery pictures, and social media uploads. Transparent PNG pixels are filled with a clean white backdrop.
