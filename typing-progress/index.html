Quantitative Typing Analytics & Performance Tracking

Tracking your typing metrics over time provides the motivation and objective feedback needed to break through performance plateaus. When practicing daily, day-to-day fluctuations in speed are normal due to fatigue, passage difficulty, and finger warmup. A longitudinal progress analytics dashboard smooths out daily noise, illustrating your true upward trajectory.

Key metrics tracked in our analytics dashboard include Net Words Per Minute, Gross WPM, Accuracy Percentage, Total Keystrokes Logged, Practice Time, and Milestone Achievements (such as reaching 50 WPM, 75 WPM, or 100 WPM with 98% accuracy). Reviewing historical charts helps you identify optimal practice times and evaluate the effectiveness of different keyboard layouts.

In strict accordance with EcrioX's privacy-first architecture, all typing test history, scores, and analytics are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage and IndexedDB. No performance records or personal metrics are ever transmitted to or stored on remote cloud databases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my typing speed test history stored?

All historical test scores and metrics are saved locally inside your web browser's secure client-side storage. Your data remains entirely private and never leaves your computer.

Will my progress history be lost if I close my browser tab?

No. Local storage persists across browser restarts and computer reboots. Your history will remain intact as long as you do not manually clear your browser's site data.

Can I reset my history to start fresh?

Yes. The dashboard includes a 'Reset History' button that allows you to clear past records and begin tracking from scratch whenever you wish.
