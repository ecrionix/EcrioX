15 Interactive Chemistry Calculators

Comprehensive, mathematically verified chemistry solvers for high school, college, and engineering students. Calculate solution molarities, empirical formulas, titration end points, gas law parameters, and Faraday yields with step-by-step mathematical proofs.

Molarity Molality Molecular Mass Percent Composition Dilution (M₁V₁=M₂V₂) pH & pOH Empirical Formula Molecular Formula Stoichiometry Limiting Reagent Percent Yield Ideal Gas Law Half-Life Faraday's Law
1. Molarity Calculator (M = n / V) Concentration

Calculates the molar concentration of a chemical solution given solute mass, molar mass, and solution volume.

2. Molality Calculator (m = n / kg solvent) Colligative

Determines molal concentration, independent of temperature expansions.

3. Molecular Mass & Molar Mass Calculator Formula Parser

Parses chemical formulas with brackets and hydrates (e.g. Ca3(PO4)2, CuSO4*5H2O, C6H12O6).

4. Percent Composition Calculator (% by Mass) Stoichiometry

Calculates the percentage mass contribution of each constituent element.

5. Dilution Calculator (C₁V₁ = C₂V₂) Lab Preparation

Leave exactly ONE field empty to solve for that missing variable.

6. pH & pOH Calculator Acids & Bases

Calculates pH, pOH, and complementary ion concentrations from [H⁺] or [OH⁻].

7. Empirical Formula Calculator Composition

Finds the simplest integer atom ratio from mass or percentage compositions.

8. Molecular Formula Calculator Structure

Scales the empirical formula by molar mass: (Empirical)n = Molecular Formula.

9. Reaction Stoichiometry Calculator Reactions

Converts moles and masses between reactants and products using stoichiometric coefficients.

10. Limiting Reagent Calculator Yield

Identifies the reactant consumed first and computes theoretical product limits.

11. Percent Yield Calculator Efficiency

Calculates synthetic efficiency: % Yield = (Actual Yield / Theoretical Yield) × 100%.

12. Ideal Gas Law Calculator (PV = nRT) Thermodynamics

Solves for Pressure, Volume, Moles, or Temperature. Leave exactly ONE field empty.

13. Radioactive Half-Life & Nuclear Kinetics Calculator Kinetics

Computes radioactive decay: N(t) = N₀ · (1/2)t / t½.

14. Faraday's Law of Electrolysis Calculator Electrochemistry

Calculates electrodeposited mass: m = (M · I · t) / (n · F), where F = 96,485 C/mol.
