Earning an Official Typing Speed Certificate

A verified typing speed certificate provides tangible proof of your keyboard competency for employment applications, freelance profiles, administrative recruitment, and academic credentials. Many employers in data entry, customer service, medical billing, executive assistance, and transcription require candidates to verify their typing speed before scheduling interviews.

The EcrioX Typing Certificate is earned by completing a standard timed typing test (1, 2, or 5 minutes) with a minimum speed of 25 Net Words Per Minute and at least 90% accuracy. The certificate records your official Net WPM, Gross WPM, Accuracy Percentage, Test Duration, Date of Completion, and a unique cryptographic Verification ID generated locally in your browser.

Once earned, you can instantly download your certificate as a high-resolution, vector-crisp PDF document formatted with professional guilloche security borders, official typography, and verification metadata. You can attach this PDF directly to job applications, embed it into your digital resume, or share it on professional networks like LinkedIn.
