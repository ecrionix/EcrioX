Why Browser-Based AI Guarantees 100% Privacy

Traditional online background removers require you to upload your personal photos, ID documents, or proprietary product photos to remote cloud servers. Those servers store, process, and occasionally retain user imagery.

EcrioX uses state-of-the-art WebAssembly (WASM) and WebGPU technologies via Transformers.js. The AI model weights are temporarily loaded into your browser's local cache. All mathematical matrix operations, pixel segmentation, and alpha blending happen locally on your device's GPU or CPU. Your images never leave your computer or smartphone.
