How to Split a PDF Online with EcrioX

  1. Upload your PDF: Drag and drop your file into the workspace or click "Select PDF File".
  2. Choose your extraction method:
    • Extract Selected Pages: Click the visual page previews or type ranges (e.g. `1-4, 7, 10`) to combine specific pages into one new PDF.
    • Split Every Page: Automatically break each page into its own individual PDF and bundle them into a convenient ZIP archive.
  3. Click "Extract": The client-side engine copies page objects and structure in milliseconds.
  4. Download: Instantly save your new document or ZIP file directly to your computer or phone.

Zero Quality Loss: Native Object Copying

When you split a PDF using EcrioX, pages are not re-encoded or flattened. The underlying PDF engine executes direct, native object copying (`copyPages`). This ensures:

  • Vector Sharpness: Fonts, logos, charts, and architectural drawings stay 100% crisp at any zoom level.
  • Selectable Text: All text remains searchable and copyable.
  • Embedded Resources: Images, annotations, and form structures on the extracted pages remain intact.

Privacy & File Security

Because EcrioX runs entirely inside your web browser via client-side JavaScript and Web Workers, your confidential invoices, legal agreements, and transcripts never touch an external server. You retain complete privacy with zero data exposure.
