Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Discover how EcrioX protects your personal data and preserves your privacy.

1. Introduction

Welcome to EcrioX ("we," "our," or "the Platform"), accessible at https://ecriox.com/. Your privacy is a core architectural priority. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected when you browse our website, how we utilize analytics and advertising technologies, and how our client-side computing paradigm safeguards your uploaded documents, images, and text.

2. Client-Side Processing & File Privacy

Unlike conventional online utility platforms that transmit your files to remote servers for processing:

  • Local Browser Execution: Active utilities on EcrioX (including our Image Compressor and Text Analyzer) operate 100% inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, and client-side JavaScript.
  • No Server Uploads: Files, photos, or text snippets you supply for processing never leave your device. They are not transmitted across the internet, saved on any server, or stored in any database.
  • Zero Data Retention: As soon as you refresh or close your browser tab, memory associated with your files is immediately released by your browser. We have zero access to the content of your files.

3. Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager (Container ID: GTM-PQFRBJ9F) and Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-N31KL5T2VY) to understand aggregated website traffic, popular utility categories, and page performance.

  • Aggregated Metrics: Google Analytics collects pseudonymous data such as device type, screen resolution, referring website, approximate geographic region (country/city level), and pages visited.
  • Privacy Safeguard: We strictly program our analytics implementation never to capture or transmit filenames, file contents, personal identifiers, passwords, or email addresses.
  • Opt-Out: You can prevent Google Analytics from tracking your visits across the web by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Advertising Technologies & Google AdSense Compliance

To maintain EcrioX as a free service without charging subscriptions, we may display online advertisements via third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to EcrioX and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

EcrioX uses minimal storage technologies:

  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage solely to remember your UI preference (e.g., Light Mode or Dark Mode). This contains no personally identifiable information.
  • Analytics/Advertising Cookies: Cookies set by Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and advertising networks to evaluate website usage and ad delivery as detailed above.

6. Communications & Inquiries

If you contact us through our website contact form or directly via email, we receive your name, email address, and message text. This information is used strictly to respond to your inquiry and is never sold, leased, or added to marketing newsletters.

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you hold legal rights regarding personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or delete any personal information we retain, as well as the right to restrict or object to certain processing activities. Since we do not require user accounts or retain user files, we store negligible identifiable personal data.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect platform improvements, new tools, or evolving legal standards. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated modification date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or privacy concerns regarding this policy, please reach out via our Contact Form or email us at contact@ecriox.com.
