Why Convert PNG to WebP for Modern Websites?

Developed by Google, WebP is a state-of-the-art modern image format designed specifically for the web. While PNG has historically been the primary choice for web graphics needing transparent backgrounds, PNG files often suffer from bulky file weights that hurt page load speeds.

Key benefits of converting your PNG images to WebP:

  • Boost Core Web Vitals & SEO: Large images are the #1 cause of poor Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) scores in Google PageSpeed Insights. Smaller WebP files load 3x faster.
  • Preserve Full Transparency: WebP supports 8-bit alpha channels just like PNG, ensuring smooth drop shadows and transparent product cutouts without white halos.
  • Universal Browser Compatibility: WebP is supported natively by over 97% of global browsers, including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and Android/iOS browsers.
