How to Extract Text from Images with Free Online OCR

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is the technological process of converting visual letterforms from photographs, paper scans, and screenshots into machine-readable digital text characters. Whether you need to extract text from a receipt, copy information from an unselectable infographic, or transcribe scanned textbook pages, EcrioX delivers instant in-browser transcription without software installation.

3

Copy or Download Text

Inspect the extracted text in the live editor, fix any terms, and download a .TXT file or copy to clipboard.

Tips for Achieving Maximum OCR Accuracy

  • Ensure Sharp Focus: Blurry or unfocused camera photos cause letters like 'c', 'e', and 'o' to blend together. Keep camera lenses stable.
  • Boost Contrast: Use our built-in High Contrast and Black & White filters to eliminate textured background paper noise.
  • Straighten Orientations: If a smartphone document is rotated sideways, click the Rotate button so words read horizontally left-to-right.
  • Select the Correct Language: When transcribing non-English text, select the appropriate trained language model from the dropdown.
