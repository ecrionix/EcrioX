Effortless Text Formatting and List Clean-Up

Whether you are drafting articles, formatting academic essays, preparing keyword spreadsheets for marketing, or cleaning lists of data entries, repetitive text tasks consume valuable hours.

EcrioX text tools provide instant, client-side solutions that operate straight in your web browser. With zero delays, zero account sign-ups, and 100% confidentiality, you can analyze, transform, deduplicate, and generate text with total peace of mind.

Popular Text Operations

  • Word & Character Counting: Calculate precise word totals, character counts with and without spaces, syllable estimates, and approximate speaking/reading durations.
  • Case Transformation: Instantly convert text blocks into UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case.
  • Data Deduplication: Eliminate duplicate list entries, clean customer records, and sort lines alphabetically in ascending or descending order.
  • Typography Prototyping: Generate realistic placeholder paragraphs to test page layouts, typography line-heights, and responsive font scaling.
