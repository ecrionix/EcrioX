The History and Purpose of Lorem Ipsum

Lorem Ipsum has served as the printing and typesetting industry's standard placeholder text since the 1500s. When an unknown printer scrambled a galley of type to assemble a specimen book, it survived five centuries and transitioned seamlessly into digital publishing.

Contrary to common belief, Lorem Ipsum is not simply random nonsense. It traces its roots to classical Latin literature from 45 BC, specifically sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of Cicero's philosophical treatise De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum ("On the Ends of Good and Evil").

Why Use Placeholder Text in Web Design?

It is a well-established cognitive phenomenon that when readers examine the visual layout of a page, their attention is naturally distracted by readable content. Using readable English or localized copy causes clients and reviewers to evaluate typography, colors, and layout through the lens of specific phrasing rather than visual hierarchy.

Lorem Ipsum creates a natural-looking distribution of letters and word lengths, closely modeling the texture of genuine editorial prose without distracting readers from visual design decisions.
