Client-Side Audio Digital Signal Processing

Audio files are typically large, making uploading them to cloud converters slow and privacy-invasive. Many remote converters compress or re-encode your sound files with lossy codecs that degrade audio fidelity.

EcrioX utilizes the browser's native Web Audio API to decode the audio stream into raw 32-bit floating point PCM audio buffers. This enables precise peak analysis, waveform visualization, dynamic range adjustment, and clean uncompressed WAV export—all running locally on your hardware.

Audio Parameters Explained

  • Sample Rate: The number of audio snapshots taken per second, measured in Hertz (Hz). Standard CD quality is 44,100 Hz (44.1 kHz), while modern video and studio audio commonly uses 48,000 Hz.
  • Channels: Mono (1 channel) delivers identical audio to both ears, while Stereo (2 channels) creates spatial separation between left and right speakers.
  • Waveform Dynamics: The visual amplitude representation indicates dynamic headroom and detects digital clipping (when peaks exceed 0 dBFS).
