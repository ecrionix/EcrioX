In-Browser Media Processing Powered by HTML5

Traditional online media tools require users to upload massive voice recordings, sound samples, and images to remote servers before processing can begin. This approach leads to long upload times, server timeouts, and potential privacy violations.

EcrioX harnesses modern browser technologies—including HTML5 Canvas and the Web Audio API—to perform digital signal processing directly on your local device. Whether you are analyzing audio sample rates or compressing high-resolution photos, your media never leaves your machine.

Capabilities

  • Audio Signal Inspection: Inspect channel counts, sample frequencies (44.1 kHz, 48 kHz), audio length, and visual peak amplitude envelopes.
  • Volume Amplification: Boost quiet speech recordings or normalize audio volume with real-time waveform monitoring.
  • Lossless Conversion: Export audio as standard uncompressed 16-bit PCM WAV audio files.
