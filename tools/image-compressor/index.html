How to Compress Images with EcrioX

  1. Select or Drop Image: Click the dropzone or drag your JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo into the workspace.
  2. Adjust Compression Quality: Use the quality slider to find your preferred balance between file size and visual sharpness. (80% is ideal for web usage).
  3. Choose Format & Resize: Convert to modern WebP for higher compression or enter a maximum pixel width to downscale large dimensions.
  4. Download Instantly: Click "Download Image" to save the optimized file directly to your device.

Why In-Browser Image Optimization Matters

Most conventional online tools require you to upload your sensitive personal photos, design assets, or documents to an external cloud server. EcrioX uses modern HTML5 Canvas rendering APIs directly inside your browser. This brings two transformative benefits:

  • Total Confidentiality: Your files remain on your local device. No network packets containing your photos are ever transmitted to any backend server.
  • Zero Waiting Time: Compression is virtually instantaneous since you don't have to wait for large uploads or downloads over slow network connections.

Supported Formats and Limitations

This utility supports JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and WebP (.webp) formats. Because processing is executed using your device's native browser memory, performance depends on your local hardware. For best results on mobile devices, images under 50 megapixels are recommended.
