How Exact Chronological Age Is Calculated

Calculating chronological age requires more than simply subtracting the birth year from the current year, because calendar months have varying lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years insert an extra day into February every four years.

The EcrioX age calculation algorithm accounts for:

  • Variable Month Durations: When borrowing days across months, the exact number of calendar days in the prior month is utilized.
  • Leap Years: Full 366-day leap year cycles under the Gregorian calendar standard.
  • Time Zones: Local calendar boundaries to prevent off-by-one errors caused by UTC offsets.
