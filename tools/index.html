Frequently Asked Questions About EcrioX Tools

Are all online tools on EcrioX completely free to use?

Yes, every digital tool and utility across EcrioX is 100% free with no subscription tiers, no credit card requirements, no paywalls, and no watermark stamps on your generated files.

Do I need to create an account or install any software?

No account registration or login is required. All utilities run directly inside your web browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without installing browser extensions or executable software.

How does EcrioX protect my files and personal privacy?

EcrioX utilizes client-side WebAssembly, JavaScript, and HTML5 Canvas to execute PDF processing, image optimization, and data calculations locally inside your browser memory. Your confidential files are never transmitted to or stored on remote cloud servers.

Can I suggest a new tool or report an issue with an existing utility?

Yes. We actively expand our utility suite based on user requests. Visit our Contact & Support page or email elango369elango@gmail.com with your feedback or feature suggestions.
