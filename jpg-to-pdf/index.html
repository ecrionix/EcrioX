How to Convert JPG and Photos to PDF with EcrioX

  1. Upload your photos: Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP images into the workspace or click "Select Image Files".
  2. Customize Page Layout: Select standard A4, US Letter, or "Fit to Image". Choose auto-orientation to allow landscape and portrait photos to sit naturally on their own pages without black bars.
  3. Arrange Page Order: Use the arrow buttons to position photos into your preferred storytelling or catalog order.
  4. Click "Convert to PDF": The client-side engine embeds images into a standardized document structure.
  5. Download: Instantly save the unified PDF directly to your device.

Aspect Ratio Preservation & Zero Distortion

Many basic image converters stretch photos to force them into a square or rectangular template, distorting faces and skewing typography. EcrioX calculates exact mathematical aspect ratios for every individual photo. Using the "Contain" setting, your photos retain their precise proportions, centered elegantly on the page.

Supported Formats Beyond JPG

While titled JPG to PDF for clarity, this converter supports JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and modern WebP (.webp) files. Transparent PNG backgrounds are automatically rendered against clean white document backdrops.
