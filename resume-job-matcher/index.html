💡 Tailoring Recommendations

How to Tailor Your Resume for ATS Screening (Without Fabricating Skills)

When an enterprise receives hundreds of submissions for a single opening, hiring teams filter applications based on required skills and credentials. Tailoring your resume does not mean inventing experience; it means framing your authentic achievements using the terminology preferred by the employer.

Align Acronyms with Full Names

Different ATS configurations may search for either the abbreviation or the spelled-out term. Use both where natural: for example, Amazon Web Services (AWS) or Search Engine Optimization (SEO).

Place Keywords in Context

A skill listed in an experience bullet point demonstrates practical hands-on capability, carrying more weight with human interviewers than a lone word in a skills list.

Never Keyword-Stuff in White Font

An old internet rumor suggested pasting the entire job description in tiny white font. Modern ATS extract text stripped of styles, displaying the hidden text directly to the recruiter and resulting in instant disqualification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good match rate on this tool?

A match rate of 70% or higher is considered strong. Job descriptions typically include "nice-to-have" skills that are not strictly essential for landing an initial screening call.
