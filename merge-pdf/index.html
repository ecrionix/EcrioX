How to Merge Multiple PDF Files with EcrioX

  1. Upload your PDF files: Select or drop 2 or more PDF documents into the workspace.
  2. Arrange the document order: Use the up and down arrow buttons to position documents exactly in your intended sequence.
  3. Click "Merge Documents": The browser engine binds pages together while preserving fonts, vector graphics, and original page orientations.
  4. Download: Your unified PDF document is generated instantaneously for download.

Preserving Page Dimensions & Formatting

A common pitfall with generic PDF mergers is that they force all documents into a single rigid page size (such as forcing US Letter pages into A4 or landscape diagrams into portrait mode).

EcrioX preserves the unique coordinate systems, rotation flags (`/Rotate`), and bounding boxes (`/MediaBox`, `/CropBox`) of every single incoming page. Whether combining an A4 invoice, an architectural blueprint, and a US Letter report, each page retains its authentic dimensions.

Privacy & Security: 100% In-Browser Execution

Legal agreements, financial contracts, and personal records should never be uploaded to unknown third-party cloud servers. EcrioX merges documents locally inside your web browser. No document data is transmitted over the internet, and no copies are stored on external infrastructure.
