What is an Online PDF Editor?

The Portable Document Format (PDF) was developed to preserve fixed layout geometry across every screen and printer. However, editing a PDF traditionally demanded costly desktop installations like Adobe Acrobat Pro. EcrioX provides a professional, full-featured online PDF editor that runs directly in your web browser. From drafting contracts and filling tax forms to signing invoices and redacting private identifiers, you can perform complete document management without software installations.

Electronic Signatures vs Digital Signatures

EcrioX provides an advanced Electronic Signature (e-Sign) suite that lets you draw your signature by hand, type your name using realistic cursive calligraphy fonts, or upload a digital signature scan with automatic background transparency removal.

Under major regulations like the US ESIGN Act and EU eIDAS regulations, electronic signatures placed on commercial agreements, nondisclosure agreements (NDAs), invoices, and employment paperwork carry legitimate legal validity for everyday business transactions.

Redaction and Document Privacy

When redacting sensitive personal identification numbers (SSNs, Aadhaar, tax IDs, credit cards), placing a black rectangle in a word processor can often be reversed by simply copying the text underneath. EcrioX PDF Editor burns redaction rectangles directly into the PDF coordinate stream, shielding confidential information from unauthorized inspection.
