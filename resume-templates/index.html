How to Choose the Right Resume Template for Your Career

The visual presentation of your resume must align with the cultural expectations of your target industry. While tech startups and creative design agencies appreciate clean monospace tags or tasteful accent bars, traditional banking, legal, and government organizations expect conservative single-column serif layouts.

When to Use Classic ATS

If you are applying to Fortune 500 corporations, government contractors, or through automated job boards like Workday and Taleo, choose Classic Professional or Modern ATS.

When to Use Tech & Engineering

Developers and engineers should showcase their repositories and technology stack prominently. The Software Engineer and Developer Portfolio templates put code competencies and live project links front and center.

When to Use Executive Pro

Candidates with 10+ years of experience, directors, and managers should use Executive Professional. Its shaded summary block immediately highlights business transformation and revenue scale.
