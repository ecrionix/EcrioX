Demystifying Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

An ATS does not possess artificial consciousness; it is fundamentally a structured document parser and keyword indexing database. When you submit your resume through online recruitment portals, the software attempts to unpack your text into standardized database fields: Candidate Name, Contact Coordinates, Degree, Job Titles, and Skill Keywords.

Why Multi-Column Layouts Fail

Older parser engines read documents strictly from left-to-right across the horizontal plane. Multi-column resumes often cause lines from different columns to merge horizontally, resulting in garbled text like combining your phone number with your job title.

Avoid Icons for Contact Details

Using miniature phone or envelope graphic icons instead of textual labels ("Phone:", "Email:") can cause parsers to miss contact information completely. Explicit textual labels are always the safest option.

Quantified Achievements Matter

Recruiters prioritize candidates whose bullets demonstrate measurable results (e.g., percentages, latencies, volume of tasks, team sizes). Where truthful, include metrics to validate your contributions.
