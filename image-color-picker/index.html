Understanding Color Codes: HEX, RGB, HSL & CMYK

When designing websites, mobile apps, or print collateral, matching exact colors across screens and mediums is essential. Each digital color space serves distinct purposes:

  • HEX (#RRGGBB): A 6-digit hexadecimal notation representing Red, Green, and Blue intensity from 00 to FF. It is the gold standard for CSS, HTML, and web design.
  • sRGB (Red, Green, Blue): An additive color model specifying light intensity from 0 to 255. Ideal for digital displays, canvas drawing, and graphic rendering.
  • HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness): A cylindrical coordinate model that mimics human perception of color. Changing lightness or saturation preserves the base chromatic hue, making HSL ideal for generating UI hover and active states.
  • CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key/Black): A subtractive four-color printing model used by commercial offset and digital presses.
