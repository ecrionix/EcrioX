How to Add Page Numbers to PDF Files Online

Numbering PDF documents is vital for academic papers, legal briefs, corporate proposals, and technical documentation. Paginated documents ensure smooth review meetings, cross-referencing, and print organization.

With the EcrioX PDF Page Numbering utility, you can paginate documents in three easy steps:

  1. Select or Drag & Drop Your PDF: Your file is rendered directly inside your browser without any network delays.
  2. Customize Placement & Styling: Pick your preferred header or footer position (such as Bottom Center or Bottom Right), formatting style ("Page X of Y"), font family, and choose whether to skip the title page.
  3. Click 'Add Page Numbers & Download': The PDF is immediately re-compiled and downloaded directly to your device.
